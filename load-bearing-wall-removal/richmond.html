Understanding Richmond's Period Properties

The London Borough of Richmond upon Thames boasts one of the finest collections of period architecture in the capital. The borough's building heritage spans several centuries, and each architectural era presents its own set of considerations when it comes to structural alterations.

Georgian Properties in Richmond

Richmond's Georgian properties, many of which date from the 18th century, are among the most architecturally significant homes in the borough. Found predominantly around Richmond Green, Maids of Honour Row, and parts of the Hill, these homes feature solid brick walls with lime mortar, timber floor joists often spanning considerable distances, and elegant proportions that homeowners are understandably keen to preserve. Removing a load bearing wall in a Georgian property requires exceptional care. The structural engineer must account for the age and condition of the masonry, the load paths through the building, and the potential impact on decorative features such as cornicing, ceiling roses, and panelling. We frequently work with conservation officers at Richmond Borough Council to ensure that any structural work respects the heritage value of these remarkable buildings.

Victorian Houses in Richmond

The Victorian era saw substantial residential development across Richmond, Twickenham, Teddington, and the surrounding areas. Victorian terraced and semi-detached houses typically feature a central load bearing wall running from front to back, dividing the ground floor into separate reception rooms. This wall is one of the most commonly removed structural elements in Richmond renovation projects, as homeowners seek to combine a cramped front parlour and rear dining room into a single, generous living space. Victorian properties in Richmond present their own challenges, including shallow foundations, rubble-filled cavity walls, and chimney breasts that may share the same structural wall. Our structural engineers are experienced in designing steel beam solutions that safely redistribute loads while preserving the Victorian character that makes these homes so appealing.

Edwardian and Inter-War Properties

Many properties in areas such as East Sheen, Barnes, and parts of Hampton were built during the Edwardian period and the inter-war years. These homes often feature wider plot widths and more generous internal dimensions than their Victorian predecessors, but they still rely on load bearing walls to support upper floors and roof structures. Removing these walls to create flowing ground-floor layouts is an increasingly popular renovation choice among Richmond homeowners, and our team has extensive experience with these property types.

The Structural Engineering Process for Wall Removal in Richmond

Every load bearing wall removal project we undertake in Richmond follows a rigorous, methodical process designed to ensure safety, structural integrity, and full compliance with building regulations.

Initial Site Survey and Assessment

Our structural engineer will visit your Richmond property to conduct a thorough assessment. During this visit, we examine the wall you wish to remove, inspect the floors above and below, check for any services running through the wall (plumbing, electrics, gas), assess the foundation type and condition, and determine the exact loads that the wall currently carries. This site survey typically takes between one and two hours, depending on the complexity of the property.

Structural Calculations and Beam Design

Following the site survey, our engineers produce detailed structural calculations in accordance with British Standards and Eurocodes. These calculations determine the size, type, and specification of the steel beam (commonly referred to as an RSJ, or Rolled Steel Joist) needed to replace the load bearing wall. For a typical Victorian terrace in Richmond, this might be a 203x133mm universal beam for a shorter span, or a larger 254x146mm or even 305x165mm section for wider openings. The beam must be sized to carry all dead loads (the weight of the building structure above), live loads (occupants, furniture), and any imposed loads, with appropriate safety factors applied.

Building Regulations Submission to Richmond Borough Council

All load bearing wall removal projects in Richmond must comply with the Building Regulations 2010 (as amended). You have two options for demonstrating compliance to the Richmond Borough Council building control department. The first is a Building Notice, which is a simpler process where you notify the council before work starts, and they inspect the work at key stages. The second is a Full Plans Application, where you submit detailed drawings and structural calculations for approval before work commences. We generally recommend the Full Plans route for Richmond properties, particularly for period homes, as it provides certainty that your proposals meet all requirements before construction begins. Our team handles all submissions on your behalf, liaising directly with Richmond's building control officers to secure approvals efficiently.

RSJ and Steel Beam Installation in Richmond Properties

The installation of an RSJ or steel beam is the central element of any load bearing wall removal project. Our experienced installation teams follow a carefully planned sequence of operations to ensure the work is completed safely and to the highest standards.

Temporary Support with Acrow Props

Before any demolition begins, we install temporary support using adjustable steel Acrow props and strongboy brackets or needles. These props are positioned on both sides of the wall to be removed, transferring the load from the floor and roof structure above to the foundations below. In Richmond's older properties, we take particular care to protect original timber floors and ensure that the props bear onto adequate foundations or spreader plates. The temporary support remains in place throughout the demolition and beam installation process, typically for several days.

Wall Demolition and Beam Fitting

With temporary support securely in place, the load bearing wall is carefully demolished. In many Richmond homes, this reveals interesting features of the original construction, from Victorian rubble fill to Georgian hand-made bricks. Once the wall is removed, the steel beam is lifted into position and secured onto padstones or concrete bearing pads at each end. The beam must bear onto solid masonry or purpose-built piers with sufficient bearing area to transfer loads safely to the foundations. Our teams ensure precise levelling and alignment before the beam is permanently fixed in place.

Making Good and Finishing

After the beam is installed and the temporary props removed, we complete all making-good works including plastering around the beam, patching floor surfaces, and preparing walls for decoration. Many homeowners in Richmond choose to box in the steel beam with plasterboard to create a clean, seamless appearance, while others prefer to leave the beam exposed as an architectural feature. We accommodate either preference and can also install downlighters within a boxed beam enclosure for additional design impact.

Listed Building Considerations in Richmond

The London Borough of Richmond upon Thames contains a significant number of listed buildings and conservation areas. Richmond Green, parts of Richmond Hill, Petersham, and numerous other locations within the borough include Grade I, Grade II*, and Grade II listed properties. If your home is listed, additional considerations and permissions apply to any structural work, including load bearing wall removal.

Listed Building Consent must be obtained from Richmond Borough Council before any work that affects the character or special interest of a listed building. This applies to both external and internal alterations, meaning that removing an internal load bearing wall in a listed property requires formal consent. The application process involves submitting detailed drawings showing existing and proposed arrangements, a heritage impact assessment explaining how the work will affect the building's significance, and structural engineering details demonstrating that the work can be carried out without harming the building's fabric.

At ProStruct Design, we have extensive experience working with listed buildings throughout Richmond. We understand the sensitivity required when proposing structural alterations to heritage properties and work closely with conservation officers to develop solutions that achieve your goals while respecting the building's historical importance. In some cases, we may recommend alternative approaches such as creating a wider opening with retained piers rather than full wall removal, or using concealed flitch beams that minimise visible impact on the interior.

Frequently Asked Questions About Load Bearing Wall Removal in Richmond

How do I know if a wall in my Richmond property is load bearing?

In Richmond's Georgian and Victorian properties, load bearing walls typically run perpendicular to floor joists and are positioned directly above foundations. Thicker walls (usually 9 inches or more) are more likely to be structural. However, the only safe way to confirm is through a structural survey by a qualified engineer. We offer assessments across Richmond to determine which walls are load bearing before any work begins.

Do I need planning permission to remove a load bearing wall in Richmond?

Most internal load bearing wall removals in Richmond do not require planning permission as they fall under permitted development. However, you must comply with building regulations, which means either submitting a building notice or full plans application to Richmond Borough Council. If your property is listed or in a conservation area, additional permissions may be required. Our team guides you through the entire approvals process.

How much does load bearing wall removal cost in Richmond?

Load bearing wall removal in Richmond typically costs between £1,800 and £7,500. This includes structural engineer fees (£350–£700), builder costs for demolition and RSJ installation, building control fees, and making good. Costs vary depending on wall length, property type, and the size of steel beam required. Georgian and Victorian properties may cost more due to their construction complexity.

What is involved in removing a load bearing wall in a listed building in Richmond?

Richmond has many listed buildings, particularly around Richmond Green and along the river. Removing a load bearing wall in a listed building requires Listed Building Consent from Richmond Borough Council in addition to building regulations approval. The work must preserve the building's character, and you may need to use sympathetic materials. Our team has experience navigating the additional requirements for listed properties in Richmond.

How long does a load bearing wall removal take in a typical Richmond home?

A standard load bearing wall removal in a Richmond property takes approximately 5 to 10 days of on-site work. This includes installing temporary Acrow prop support, demolishing the wall, fitting the RSJ or steel beam, and making good with plastering and decoration. The overall project timeline, including structural calculations and building control approval, is usually 4 to 8 weeks from initial consultation to completion.

Can I remove a load bearing wall between my kitchen and dining room in Richmond?

Yes, creating an open-plan kitchen-diner by removing the load bearing wall between these rooms is one of the most popular projects we undertake in Richmond. A structural engineer will design a suitable steel beam (RSJ) to transfer the load safely. This type of project significantly enhances the living space and can add considerable value to Richmond properties, where open-plan layouts are highly sought after.
